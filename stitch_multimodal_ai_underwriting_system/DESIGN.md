## Brand & Style
The design system reflects institutional authority, actuarial precision, and cognitive clarity. Engineered specifically for complex risk decisioning, algorithmic underwriting, and forensic policy analysis, the interface instills confidence through deterministic visual structures and high-density information architecture.

The visual style blends **Corporate Modern** rigor with **Technical Minimalism**:
- **High-Density Utility**: Prioritizes signal-to-noise ratio, enabling underwriters to parse multi-page risk dossiers, anomaly graphs, and predictive confidence scores without unnecessary scroll or visual friction.
- **Surgical Precision**: Employs razor-thin dividing borders, structured tabular views, and strict vertical alignments that evoke technical schematics, high-frequency trading terminals, and analytical workbenches.
- **Calm, High-Acuity Substrate**: Leans on deep slate surfaces and subtle tonal shifts to reduce ocular fatigue over extended 8-hour review sessions, reserving saturated chroma exclusively for triage states, algorithmic anomalies, and decision imperatives.

## Colors
The color palette establishes an uncompromising chromatic hierarchy tailored for risk triage:

### Structural Canvas & Neutrals
- **Base Canvas (Slate 950)**: `#0B0F19` – Extreme low-glare foundation.
- **Surface Primary (Slate 900)**: `#0F172A` – Primary workspace, dossier container panels, and navigation spine.
- **Surface Secondary (Slate 800)**: `#1E293B` – Elevated cards, data cells, input fields, and modal containers.
- **Surface Muted / Hover (Slate 700/50)**: `#334155` – Selected rows, interactive hover states, and structural dividers.
- **High-Contrast Canvas Light (Fallback / Light Mode)**: `#F8FAFC` for high-key executive export sheets.

### Semantic Triage & Verification Signals
- **Primary / Intelligence (`#6366F1`)**: Indigo. Dedicated to AI inference vectors, computer vision bounding boxes, extraction masks, and copilot dialogue threads.
- **Low Risk / Automated Approval (`#10B981`)**: Emerald. Applied to cleared guidelines, high-confidence extractions (>95%), and pristine risk factors.
- **Moderate Risk / Human Referral (`#F59E0B`)**: Amber. Dedicated to sensory discrepancies, flagged exclusions, manual underwriting triggers, and medium-confidence metrics.
- **High Risk / Non-Conforming / Decline (`#EF4444`)**: Crimson. Reserved for policy breaches, catastrophic risk concentrations, fraud flags, and auto-declines.

### Text & Accents
- **Text High-Emphasis**: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
- **Text Medium-Emphasis**: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400)
- **Text Low-Emphasis / Metadata**: `#64748B` (Slate 500)
- **Structural Border (Standard)**: `#1E293B` (1px solid)
- **Structural Border (Prominent)**: `#334155` (1px solid)

## Typography
Typography is split purposefully between structured interface narrative and deterministic computational data:

- **Primary Typeface (Inter)**: Drives narrative assessment, documentation previews, conversational agent threads, policy guidelines, and navigation. Inter provides balanced optical proportions that remain legible in tight horizontal spaces.
- **Monospace Engine (JetBrains Mono)**: Exclusively designated for numeric values, loss ratios, premium calculations, geospatial coordinates, document checksums, API payload citations, confidence percentages, and discrepancy deltas. Its tabular figures and tall x-height allow immediate vertical optical alignment down complex balance sheets and tabular risk spreads.

### Hierarchical Usage Rules
- All numeric data within tables and telemetry cards must implement `label-code-*` variants with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- Metric units (e.g., `USD`, `%`, `bps`) must match the font-size of their parent monospaced value but step down in opacity to 60% (`#94A3B8`).
- Section headers across underwriting tiers strictly use `headline-sm` with upper-case transformation, tracking expanded to `0.05em`, rendered in `#94A3B8`.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an extreme-density, multi-pane workbench paradigm engineered to fit full underwriting workflows across standardized corporate monitors (1440px to 4K displays).

### Grid & Panes
- **Workbench Multi-Panel**: An adjustable three-pane architecture:
  1. **Left Nav/Queue Rail (240px to 320px fixed)**: Worklist queue, triage state filters, SLA count timers.
  2. **Primary Workspace (Fluid 60-70% width)**: Tabulated applicant data, extracted unstructured documents side-by-side with computer vision boundary validations.
  3. **Copilot & Actuarial Context Rail (360px to 420px fixed)**: AI verification timeline, interactive guideline checker, manual override overrides.
- **Table Density**: Data row height strictly maintained at `32px` (compact) or `40px` (standard review), keeping padding to `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally.

### Breakpoints & Adaptation
- **Desktop Wide (>1440px)**: Three simultaneous active panels with live split-screen document verification and prompt inspection.
- **Desktop Compact (1024px - 1439px)**: Third panel (Copilot) toggles as an anchored slide-over drawer over the secondary document preview canvas.
- **Tablet / Review Standby (<1024px)**: Single unified view with high-level summary KPIs; tabs collapse horizontally into a segment controller.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this design system is conveyed via **Tonal Layering** and **Razor-Thin Outlines** rather than high-diffusion drop shadows, eliminating visual clutter in multi-window layouts.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#0B0F19` – Raw substrate underlying master frames.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Master Cards)**: `#0F172A` with a 1px solid border of `#1E293B`.
- **Level 2 (Active Data Modules, Inputs, Hover States)**: `#1E293B` bordered with `#334155`.
- **Level 3 (Popovers, Context Menus, Tooltips)**: `#1E293B` with 1px solid `#475569` border and an ultra-subtle directional shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **AI Focus Overlay (Bounding Boxes & Annotations)**: 1px continuous hairline outline (`#6366F1`) combined with an interior tint fill `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)`. Hovering or focusing an inference box lifts border luminosity to `#818CF8` with an interior fill of `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.16)`.

## Shapes
The design system enforces a disciplined geometric style (`Soft` - 0.25rem default) to support density and avoid wasted space:

- **Data Tables & Module Panels**: Precision-machined 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radius.
- **Form Inputs & Action Buttons**: 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radius.
- **Badges, Tags, & Status Pills**: 2px (`0.125rem`) micro-radius for tabular chips, maintaining crisp alignment beside monospaced figures.
- **AI Overlays & Document Crop Polygons**: 0px radius (absolute sharp rectangular coordinates) to ensure pixel-perfect fidelity with scanned loss runs and medical documents.

- **Focus State**: Border `1px solid #6366F1`, Outline `none`, Subtle glow `0 0 0 1px #6366F1`.
- **Confidence Metric Input**: Split inputs showing the extracted value adjacent to an interactive confidence meter (e.g., `[ 98.4% ]` in `label-code-md`).

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkbox**: 14px × 14px box, 2px radius, Background `#0F172A`, Border `1px solid #475569`. Checked state: Background `#6366F1`, Checkmark white stroke.
- **Radio**: 14px × 14px circle. Checked state: Border `1.5px solid #6366F1` with an inner 6px `#6366F1` filled pip.

### 6. Specialized Underwriting Components
- **CV Bounding Box Callout**: Floating rectangular target over source PDF viewer. Renders a `1px solid #6366F1` frame with an anchor label pill in top-left containing document key (`label-code-sm`) and confidence percentage.
- **Confidence Guage Strip**: Inline 32px-wide by 3px-high micro-bar indicating certainty tier (Emerald >90%, Amber 70-89%, Crimson <70%).
- **Copilot Query Dialogue**: Grounded conversation container using `#0F172A`, with left citation bar linking back to original policy section anchors via hover highlight.

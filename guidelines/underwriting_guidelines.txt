PROPERTY & CASUALTY INSURANCE UNDERWRITING GUIDELINES
Version 2.0 | Effective January 2025

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

§1. GENERAL ELIGIBILITY

§1.1 Property Types — Eligible
• Single-family dwellings (1-4 units)
• Townhouses and condominiums (owner-occupied)
• Vacation/seasonal homes (occupied ≥30 days/year)

§1.2 Property Types — Ineligible
• Commercial properties or mixed-use with >25% commercial space
• Properties with active code violations or unresolved stop-work orders
• Vacant properties (unoccupied >60 consecutive days)
• Properties in active foreclosure proceedings

§1.3 Occupancy Requirements
• Primary residences and secondary homes are eligible
• Short-term rentals (<30-day leases) require surplus lines referral
• Properties rented ≥9 months/year require landlord policy endorsement

§2. CONSTRUCTION & AGE

§2.1 Construction Type
ACCEPT: Frame, brick veneer, masonry, concrete block, log homes with treated logs
REFER: Earth construction (adobe, rammed earth), straw bale, shipping container conversions
DECLINE: Tarpaper, cardboard, or non-code construction materials

§2.2 Age & Condition
• Properties built after 1970: Standard rating
• Properties built 1950–1969: Accept if roof, electrical, plumbing, HVAC updated within 20 years
• Properties built before 1950: Require inspection report confirming system modernization

§2.3 Foundation
ACCEPT: Concrete slab, crawl space, full basement with no active water intrusion
REFER: Properties with foundation cracks >¼" width, visible settling, or bowing walls
DECLINE: Properties with active foundation failure or structural engineer red flag

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

§3. ROOF

§3.1 Age Thresholds
• Architectural shingles: ≤25 years
• 3-tab asphalt shingles: ≤20 years
• Metal, tile, slate: ≤40 years
• Flat/modified bitumen: ≤15 years

Properties with roofs exceeding age threshold require certification of serviceable condition
or replacement within 12 months of policy inception.

§3.2 Roof Condition Flags
ACCEPT: Minor wear consistent with age, no missing shingles
REFER: Moderate granule loss, <5 missing/damaged shingles, curling at edges
DECLINE: Widespread damage, exposed sheathing, sag/structural deflection, active leaks

§3.3 High-Wind & Hail Exposure
Properties in Tier 1 wind zones (coastal regions, tornado alleys) with roofs >10 years old
require wind mitigation inspection. Hail-prone regions (TX, CO, OK panhandle) with roofs
>15 years require hail-resistance certification (Class 3 or 4 impact rating) for standard rates.

§4. ELECTRICAL, PLUMBING, HVAC

§4.1 Electrical Systems
ACCEPT: Circuit breakers, copper wiring, grounded outlets, GFCI in wet areas
REFER: Knob-and-tube wiring (if less than 30% of home and not actively used)
DECLINE: Active knob-and-tube, aluminum branch wiring without remediation, Federal Pacific or Zinsco panels

§4.2 Plumbing
ACCEPT: Copper, PEX, CPVC
REFER: Galvanized steel (if ≥50 years old), polybutylene piping (requires inspection)
DECLINE: Lead pipes, polybutylene with documented leak history

§4.3 Heating Systems
ACCEPT: Central forced air, heat pump, radiant, baseboard electric
REFER: Wood stove (primary heat source), space heaters as sole heating
DECLINE: Unvented kerosene heaters, unapproved LPG systems

§5. HAZARD & ENVIRONMENTAL EXPOSURE

§5.1 Flood Zone
• Zones X, C (minimal flood hazard): Standard acceptance
• Zones A, AE, V, VE (FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area): Require National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policy; underlying property coverage accepted
• Properties with 2+ flood claims in past 10 years: DECLINE

§5.2 Wildfire Risk
• Low/Moderate risk zones: Standard
• High/Extreme risk zones: Require defensible space certification (100 ft clearance, ember-resistant vents, non-combustible siding) for standard rates; otherwise refer to surplus lines

§5.3 Earthquake
Properties in seismic zones 3–4 (CA, PacNW, AK): Standard acceptance; earthquake coverage optional
Unreinforced masonry in seismic zones: REFER for structural engineer assessment

§5.4 Proximity to Coast
• >1 mile from coastline: Standard
• ≤1 mile: Wind mitigation inspection required; properties on barrier islands or with <10 ft elevation above sea level referred to coastal underwriting unit

§6. CLAIMS HISTORY

§6.1 Prior Claims — Applicant
• 0 claims in 5 years: Standard rating
• 1 claim in 5 years: Standard with 5% increase if claim >$10k
• 2 claims in 5 years: 15% surcharge; 3+ claims → REFER
• Any liability claim >$50k: REFER

§6.2 Property Loss History (C.L.U.E.)
Properties with 2+ claims in past 7 years regardless of owner: REFER
Exception: Single weather event affecting multiple properties in area (named storm, wildfire) not held against applicant

§7. SPECIAL FEATURES & LIABILITIES

§7.1 Pools & Trampolines
• In-ground pool with 4-sided fence (≥4 ft, self-latching gate): ACCEPT
• Above-ground pool with ladder removed when not in use: ACCEPT
• Trampolines: Require safety net enclosure or liability exclusion

§7.2 Dogs & Animal Liability
ACCEPT: Most breeds with no bite history
REFER: 1 bite incident (requires liability exclusion or higher limits)
DECLINE: 2+ bite incidents, or breeds on restricted list (Pit Bull, Rottweiler, Doberman, Akita, Chow, Wolf-hybrid) without canine liability coverage from surplus carrier

§7.3 Home Business
• Incidental office use (no clients visiting, no inventory): ACCEPT
• Client-facing business (daycare, salon, therapy office): Require commercial GL policy

§7.4 Detached Structures
Detached garages, sheds, workshops ≤1000 sq ft: Included in standard coverage
Structures >1000 sq ft or with commercial use: Schedule separately with valuation

§8. COVERAGE & UNDERWRITING ACTIONS

§8.1 Decision Matrix
• APPROVE: All eligibility criteria met; standard or surcharged rates applied
• REFER: One or more discretionary factors present; requires senior underwriter or specialized unit review
• DECLINE: Ineligible property type, unacceptable risk factor, or regulatory restriction

§8.2 Information Requests
When documentation is incomplete, issue Information Request (IR) with 15-day response deadline.
Common IRs: roof certification, 4-point inspection (electrical/plumbing/HVAC/roof), wind mitigation, appraisal, engineer report

§8.3 Policy Conditions
Acceptance may include:
• Repair/replacement requirements (e.g., "Replace roof within 12 months")
• Coverage exclusions (e.g., trampoline liability exclusion)
• Increased deductibles (e.g., 2% wind/hail deductible in coastal zones)
• Mandatory endorsements (e.g., water backup, equipment breakdown)

§9. DOCUMENT INTERPRETATION GUIDANCE

§9.1 Inspection Reports
• Photos showing property exterior, roof, foundation, electrical panel, plumbing, HVAC
• Inspector comments in "Recommendations" or "Repair" sections are FLAGS — assess severity
• "Informational" or "Monitor" items are generally acceptable; "Safety Concern" or "Immediate Repair" items require remediation before binding

§9.2 Appraisal Reports
• Verify replacement cost vs. market value (replacement cost used for Coverage A)
• Note any functional obsolescence, deferred maintenance, or adverse site conditions
• Appraiser's "Subject to" conditions must be cleared before policy issuance

§9.3 Satellite & Aerial Imagery
• Roof condition: Look for discoloration, moss/debris, tarp presence (indicates leak/damage)
• Defensible space: Vegetation within 10 ft of structure in wildfire zones
• Detached structures: Count and verify usage (storage vs. occupancy)
